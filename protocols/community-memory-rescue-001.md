# Community Memory Rescue Protocol 001

When a useful online community is about to disappear:

1. **Establish the shutdown deadline and control.** Determine what is disappearing, when, and who controls the material.
2. **Inventory the corpus.** Estimate or enumerate pages, threads, posts, files, media, and other assets before capture.
3. **Capture public material in open archival formats.** Prefer formats designed for long-term preservation and later verification.
4. **Preserve provenance.** Retain original URLs, timestamps, attribution, and contextual metadata.
5. **Verify against the inventory.** A crawl is not complete because it finished without error; compare the archive to the expected corpus.
6. **Keep more than one copy.** Avoid a single point of failure and preserve integrity checks where possible.
7. **Separate preservation from permission to republish.** Capturing material for preservation does not automatically grant the right to redistribute it.
8. **Provide a searchable home and living handoff.** An archive is more useful when future people can discover, navigate, understand, and steward it.

## Principle

Preservation is not “download everything.” It is making future verification possible.
